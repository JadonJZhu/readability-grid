Readability Grid

Proof-of-concept: color-blindness verification tool for artists to check if visual effects show up against various backgrounds under different types of vision.

Effects Drop PNG or WebP with transparency, or JPG (treated as opaque). Up to 12.
Backgrounds Drop PNG, JPG or WebP. Up to 12.

Difference is a standard formula (CIEDE2000) that turns two colors into lightness, red-green and yellow-blue values and measures how far apart they are, adjusted to match how people see; bigger means easier to tell apart. The score is the share of the effect's pixels that differ from the background by at least T, after both are recolored to simulate each kind of vision.

 Share of effect pixels clearly visible: ✓ pass 70% or more, ! warn 40% to 69%, ✕ fail under 40%
